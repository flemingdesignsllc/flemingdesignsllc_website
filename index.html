Patrick Fleming is a South Carolina-based pyrographist who creates intricate, high-contrast woodburnings entirely by hand. Working with solid hardwood cut-offs like white oak, spalted maple, and poplar, he lets the timber's natural grain and organic edges shape each piece.

His style is rooted in Scherenschnitte, the historic art of fine-line paper cutting. Using a professional Razertip burner, Patrick translates those delicate, lace-like silhouettes into wood, using precise heat control to etch deep textures and tonal gradients directly into the grain.

His work explores European folklore, historical mythology, and dark nature. Focusing on stark archetypes, legendary beasts, and ancient, untamed landscapes, Patrick merges old-world craftsmanship with raw natural materials to bridge traditional folk craft and contemporary fine art.
